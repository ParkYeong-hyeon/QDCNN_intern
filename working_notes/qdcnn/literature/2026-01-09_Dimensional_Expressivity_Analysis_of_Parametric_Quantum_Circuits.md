## Summary

This paper develops a method to analyze the **dimensional expressivity** of parametric quantum circuits. The technique allows for:
- Identifying superfluous parameters in circuit layouts
- Obtaining maximally expressive ansatz with minimum number of parameters
- Using hybrid quantum-classical approach for efficient implementation
- Demonstrating proof-of-principle on IBM's quantum hardware

The method also discusses the effect of symmetries and how to incorporate or remove symmetries from parametrized ansatz.

## Key Contributions

- **Dimensional expressivity analysis method**: Technique to quantify how many independent parameters are actually needed in a parametric quantum circuit
- **Parameter reduction**: Method to identify and remove redundant parameters while maintaining expressivity
- **Hardware implementation**: Proof-of-principle demonstration on IBM quantum hardware using hybrid quantum-classical approach
- **Symmetry handling**: Discussion of how symmetries affect expressivity and methods to incorporate or remove them
