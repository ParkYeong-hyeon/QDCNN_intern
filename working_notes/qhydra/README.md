# Quantum Hydra Project Notes

This directory contains notes, literature reviews, and experimental design for the Quantum Hydra project.

## Structure

- `literature/` - Paper summaries and bibliography related to Quantum Hydra
- `insights/` - Casual insights and observations (more structured than scribble, less formal than concepts)
- `concepts/` - Concept-focused notes and summaries for Quantum Hydra
- `ideas/` - Idea bank and experiment queue for Quantum Hydra
- `experiment_design/` - Experimental design artifacts, baselines, benchmarks, and analysis plans
- `results/` - Results analysis, figures, tables, and summaries
