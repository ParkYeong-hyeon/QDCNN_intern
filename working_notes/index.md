# Working Notes

This directory contains exploratory and unfinished notes.
They are not finalized claims or implementation plans.

## Structure
- qdcnn/: notes related to the QDCNN project
- qhydra/: notes related to the Quantum Hydra project
- shared/: cross-project concepts and background thoughts(mainly abount general quantum computing)

## How to read
- Notes are organized by date (YYYY-MM-DD).
- Sections within a note are time-stamped.
- Literature notes and citations are stored separately.

## Intended use
These notes serve as a thinking space and as context
for later discussions with Cursor AI and paper writing.
