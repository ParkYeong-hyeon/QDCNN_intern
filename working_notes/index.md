# Working Notes

This directory contains exploratory and unfinished notes.
They are not finalized claims or implementation plans.

## Structure

This workspace is organized into three loosely related projects:

- **qdcnn/**: Notes related to the QDCNN (Quantum Dilated Convolutional Neural Networks) project
  - `literature/` - Paper summaries and bibliography
  - `ideas/` - Idea bank and experiment queue
  - `experiment_design/` - Experimental design artifacts

- **qhydra/**: Notes related to the Quantum Hydra project
  - `literature/` - Paper summaries and bibliography
  - `ideas/` - Idea bank and experiment queue
  - `experiment_design/` - Experimental design artifacts

- **shared/**: Cross-project concepts and background thoughts (mainly about general quantum computing)
  - `literature/` - Paper summaries and bibliography on general topics
  - `ideas/` - Shared ideas and concepts
  - `experiment_design/` - Shared experimental design patterns

- **study/**: Personal study notes on theoretical topics (classical ML, linear algebra, etc.)
  - `concepts/` - Theoretical concepts and definitions
  - `insights/` - Learning insights and observations
  - `literature/` - Papers, books, and resources studied

## How to read
- Notes are organized by date (YYYY-MM-DD).
- Sections within a note are time-stamped.
- Literature notes and citations are stored separately.

## Intended use
These notes serve as a thinking space and as context
for later discussions with Cursor AI and paper writing.
