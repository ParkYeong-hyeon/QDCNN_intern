# Personal Study Notes

This directory contains personal study notes on theoretical topics that are not immediately needed for current experiments, but are relevant for deeper understanding.

## Purpose

This folder is for studying foundational and theoretical concepts such as:
- Classical machine learning theory
- Linear algebra and mathematical foundations
- Optimization theory
- Information theory
- Other theoretical topics relevant to quantum ML research

These are **learning notes** rather than project-specific research notes.

## Structure

- `concepts/` - Theoretical concepts and definitions
- `insights/` - Learning insights and observations
- `literature/` - Papers, books, and resources studied

## Relationship to Project Notes

- **Project notes** (`qdcnn/`, `qhydra/`): Focused on specific project applications
- **Study notes** (`study/`): Focused on theoretical understanding and learning
- **Shared notes** (`shared/`): Cross-project concepts relevant to multiple projects

Study notes may eventually inform project work, but they are kept separate to maintain focus on learning rather than immediate application.
